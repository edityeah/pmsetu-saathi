# PM-SETU fund flow, KPIs and monitoring

## Escrow and tranches
Each SPV runs an escrow account at a scheduled commercial bank. Centre, State and Industry deposit their shares, and the money becomes usable only after all three have paid in for that tranche. Year 1: a single advance of up to 10% of total project outlay on SIP approval (no second instalment in Year 1). Years 2 to 5: the remaining 90% in annual instalments, each released in two tranches of 50%.

## First tranche conditions (start of each year from Year 2)
1. At least 75% of the previous release utilised, evidenced by expenditure reports and Utilisation Certificates certified by the SPV's head of finance and a Chartered Accountant.
2. The current year's Annual Operational Plan (AOP) submitted by the SPV and approved by the State Steering Committee.
3. At least 80% of the previous year's KPI targets achieved, measured cost-weighted.

## Second tranche conditions (mid-year)
1. At least 50% of the current year's KPI targets achieved by mid-year.
2. The industry partner has deposited its full share for the year.
3. The State has deposited its full share for the year.
4. Interim progress reports and financial statements submitted and prior review observations addressed.

## Cost-weighted KPI scoring
Each budget component contributes to the KPI score in proportion to its share of the budget: if equipment is 30% of cost and 80% procured, it contributes 24 points. About 10% is reserved for outcome KPIs that do not track spend: trainee performance, placement, industry satisfaction, governance.

## Financial controls
No cash transactions; all payments through PFMS with real-time reconciliation against budget lines. Interest on idle funds goes back to the Consolidated Fund of India. No new release until UCs for at least 75% of previous funds are accepted. A national integrated dashboard tracks releases and utilisation per cluster and MSDE may publish a scorecard naming slow clusters.

## Reporting
Quarterly reports to the SSC through the Independent Monitoring Agency covering operations, upgradation works, KPI compliance, enrolment, placement records and dropouts. A new Annual Plan each year, at least three months before the current one expires. The IMA reviews designs and the Annual Plan within 14 days and the State approves within 14 days, else deemed approved.

## Governance bodies
National Steering Committee (chaired by Secretary MSDE) approves SIPs and guidelines. National Project Monitoring Unit in DGT tracks DLIs and KPIs, builds LOMS, grievance tool and surveys, publishes annual field audits (seat utilisation, pass %, placement %, funds used, OJTs completed). State Steering Committee (chaired by Chief Secretary) approves AOPs and reviews quarterly. State PMU of about four people including a data analyst. Independent Monitoring Agency, appointed by the State, verifies milestones, collects KPI data, certifies completion, triggers tranche releases and inspects ITIs.

## KPIs (Annexure 7)
Seat utilisation 95%; pass percentage 90%; short-term course output +50%; placement rate above 75%; average salary +50% over baseline; women, PwD, SC/ST, rural and tribal share +50%; female placement above 75%; 10 courses upgraded per hub and 8 per spoke; 4 new CTS courses per hub and 2 per spoke; 10 new short-term courses per cluster; trainer vacancy below 10%; more than 95% of trainers upskilled; revenue from non-government sources at least 30%; 100% annual reporting. License Agreement KPIs: increase in enrolment, verified placement, female enrolment, verified salaries, training of trainers, verifiable technology adoption. The SPV must run a graduate tracer survey for all pass-outs.
